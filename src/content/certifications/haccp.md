Independently assessed by QBM and found compliant with **HACCP** — Hazard Analysis Critical
Control Point. Certification No. **QBM241059**.

HACCP is the operational layer beneath our ISO systems: the specific critical control points
in handling chilled and frozen food, the limits set at each one, and the monitoring records
that prove those limits held. It is what turns a cold-chain promise into something auditable.
