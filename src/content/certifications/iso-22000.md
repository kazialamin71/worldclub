Independently assessed by QBM and found compliant with the requirements of **ISO 22000:2018**,
the international standard for food safety management systems. Certification No. **QBM241057**.

ISO 22000 is the standard institutional buyers ask for first. It requires food-safety hazards
to be identified, controlled and verified across the entire chain we touch — receiving,
storage, production, picking and dispatch — with the evidence to prove it at audit.
