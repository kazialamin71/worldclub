Bakery moves faster than any other ambient line we carry, and it is judged on arrival
condition rather than specification. The range is picked and dispatched on a short cycle so
supershop shelves and hotel kitchens receive it well inside its shelf life, with batch and
production dates recorded on every consignment.

Bakery manufacturing already sits inside the certified scope of our ISO 22000:2018 and HACCP
systems, which is what allows the same range to be supplied to institutional buyers and Army
CSD stores without a separate vendor qualification.
