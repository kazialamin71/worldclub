Dairy demands the tightest chilled tolerance of anything we carry, and the shortest tolerance
for delay. Stock is rotated strictly first-expiry-first-out, and delivery schedules for
chilled dairy are fixed rather than on-demand so that remaining shelf life at the point of
sale stays predictable.

We hold direct representation for Philadelphia cheese from Australia and Bauer yoghurt from
Germany, which gives our retail clients brand-supported product rather than grey-market stock.
