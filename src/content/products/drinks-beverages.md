Beverages are a volume business, and volume is a storage problem before it is a sales problem.
The range runs through the same warehousing that carries our dry goods, with chilled handling
available where a buyer wants product delivered cold rather than cooled on their own shelf.

Carbonated beverages, fruit drinks and energy drinks all sit inside the certified scope of our
ISO 22000:2018 and HACCP systems, and are part of what World Club exports out of Bangladesh as
well as distributes within it.
