Electronics is a separate trading division, and it started where the food business already had
expertise: refrigeration and the equipment a Bangladeshi retailer actually needs on the shop
floor. The range has since grown into home and kitchen appliances and personal-care electronics
from Panasonic, Philips, Tefal and Moulinex.

Every line is quoted by model code, because in this category the model is the specification —
capacity, wattage and configuration all sit inside it. Product is supplied with manufacturer
warranty, import documentation and after-sales support, through the same national distribution
network that already reaches the country's supershop chains and department stores.

The ISO 22000:2018 and HACCP certifications published on this site cover the food operation and
are not claimed for this range; these lines carry manufacturer certification and import
documentation instead.
