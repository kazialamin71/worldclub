World Club began as an export business, and that side of the company still runs. Premium
Bangladeshi food products are shipped directly to buyers in **Japan, Canada, France, the
Netherlands, Malaysia and Saudi Arabia** — not through intermediaries.

For overseas buyers this means a single Bangladeshi counterparty with ISO 9001, ISO 22000 and
HACCP certification, its own production scope, export documentation experience across six
markets, and offices in the Netherlands, the UAE, Thailand and Australia.
