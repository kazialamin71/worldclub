Fresh fruit is the category World Club is best known for. Consignments arrive by air and by
refrigerated sea freight, clear customs against full phytosanitary documentation, and move
directly into temperature-controlled storage at Tejgaon or the BADC facility at Hazrat
Shahjalal International Airport.

Every lot is graded on arrival for size, colour break and defect rate before it is released.
Supershop and hotel clients receive fruit already sorted to their own specification, which is
why our produce reaches the shelf with retail-grade appearance and predictable shelf life.
