Vegetables are the most time-sensitive category we handle. Order cycles are built backwards
from the client's service day so that stock spends the minimum possible time in storage
between landing and delivery.

The range is aimed squarely at professional kitchens and premium retail: hotel and restaurant
groups buying to a fixed weekly specification, and supershop chains that need the exotic salad
wall filled consistently rather than occasionally.
