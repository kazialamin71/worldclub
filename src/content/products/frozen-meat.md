Institutional buyers — Army CSD stores, hotel groups and large retail chains — audit the paper
trail as closely as the product. Every frozen meat consignment carries its import
documentation, health certification and cold-chain record, and our HACCP system governs how it
is received, stored, picked and dispatched.

Madina is our own frozen duck label, developed to fill a gap between imported premium poultry
and what the local market could reliably supply.
