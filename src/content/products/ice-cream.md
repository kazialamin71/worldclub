Ice cream punishes any weakness in a distribution network — a single thaw-and-refreeze cycle
is visible in the tub and unsellable on the shelf. Our deep-frozen storage and refrigerated
delivery fleet exist precisely so that this category can be handled at all.

For retailers, that means an imported dessert range that arrives in the condition the brand
intended, right through the Bangladeshi summer.
