Spices sit inside the certified scope of our ISO 9001, ISO 22000 and HACCP certificates, which
covers manufacturing as well as trade. That distinction matters to retail buyers: the blend
range is produced under an audited food-safety management system, not simply repacked.

The same certified scope covers the wider dry range — chanachur, pickle, semai, vermicelli,
halim and khichuri mixes, and the puffed and flattened rice lines that also drive our export
business.
